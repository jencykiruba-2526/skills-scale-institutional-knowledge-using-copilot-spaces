# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interactions
- **With Project Manager:** Receives task assignments, provides estimates and status updates
- **With Product Manager:** Clarifies acceptance criteria and feature requirements
- **With Business Analyst:** Discusses requirements and technical constraints
- **With Solution Architect:** Implements architectural designs and participates in technical reviews
- **With Quality Assurance Lead:** Collaborates on testing strategies and defect resolution
- **With Agile Coach/Scrum Master:** Participates in ceremonies and removes impediments

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interactions
- **With Project Manager:** Aligns on priorities, timelines, and scope
- **With Developers:** Defines requirements and validates solutions
- **With Business Analyst:** Partners on market research and requirements gathering
- **With Solution Architect:** Reviews feasibility of proposed solutions
- **With Stakeholders:** Gathers input and communicates roadmap direction

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interactions
- **With Developers:** Assigns work, tracks progress, manages dependencies
- **With Product Manager:** Aligns on priorities and scope
- **With Business Analyst:** Reviews requirements and business impact
- **With Solution Architect:** Incorporates technical considerations into planning
- **With Change Management Lead:** Coordinates organizational change activities
- **With Quality Assurance Lead:** Incorporates testing schedules into project timeline
- **With Agile Coach/Scrum Master:** Partners on process execution

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts gather, analyze, and document business requirements. They bridge the gap between stakeholders' needs and technical solutions, ensuring clarity and alignment throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct stakeholder interviews and requirements gathering sessions
- Document and validate business requirements and acceptance criteria
- Analyze business processes and identify improvement opportunities
- Create requirements traceability matrices and specification documents
- Facilitate communication between business stakeholders and technical teams
- Validate solutions against business requirements

### Goals
- Ensure requirements are clear, complete, and measurable
- Minimize rework caused by unclear or misunderstood requirements
- Improve alignment between business objectives and technical solutions
- Identify business risks and opportunities early

### Typical Communication
- Requirements documentation and specification sheets
- Stakeholder interviews and workshop facilitation
- Status updates on requirement changes and impact analysis
- Acceptance criteria validation and sign-off

### Interactions
- **With Project Manager:** Provides requirements details and impact analysis for planning
- **With Product Manager:** Partners on understanding market and customer needs
- **With Developers:** Clarifies requirements and discusses technical feasibility
- **With Solution Architect:** Collaborates on solution design aligned with requirements
- **With Quality Assurance Lead:** Ensures acceptance criteria are testable
- **With Change Management Lead:** Communicates business process changes to stakeholders

---

## Solution Architect

### Role Summary
Solution Architects design technical solutions that meet business requirements while considering scalability, performance, and maintainability. They provide architectural guidance and ensure alignment between technical decisions and business goals.

### Responsibilities
- Design technical solutions that address business and functional requirements
- Evaluate technology options and recommend solutions
- Create architecture documentation and design specifications
- Conduct design reviews and provide technical guidance to development teams
- Assess technical risks and propose mitigation strategies
- Ensure architectural decisions align with organizational standards and best practices

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable, and reliable technical solutions
- Reduce technical debt and architectural risks
- Optimize for performance, security, and cost
- Enable teams to implement solutions efficiently

### Typical Communication
- Architecture design documents and diagrams
- Design review meetings and technical discussions
- Technology evaluation reports and recommendations
- Technical spike outcomes and feasibility assessments

### Interactions
- **With Developers:** Provides architectural guidance and reviews implementation
- **With Product Manager:** Understands requirements and validates technical feasibility
- **With Business Analyst:** Reviews business requirements and confirms technical alignment
- **With Project Manager:** Communicates technical complexity and timeline implications
- **With Quality Assurance Lead:** Ensures architectural decisions support testability and quality
- **With Change Management Lead:** Addresses technical implications of organizational changes

---

## Change Management Lead

### Role Summary
Change Management Leads plan and execute organizational change strategies to ensure smooth adoption of new processes, systems, and ways of working. They minimize disruption and increase stakeholder buy-in throughout the transition.

### Responsibilities
- Assess organizational readiness and impact of changes
- Develop change management plans and communication strategies
- Conduct stakeholder analysis and engagement planning
- Facilitate training and adoption activities
- Monitor change progress and adjust strategies as needed
- Address resistance and support stakeholders through transitions

### Goals
- Ensure successful adoption of changes with minimal disruption
- Build stakeholder buy-in and reduce resistance
- Maintain organizational productivity during transitions
- Embed new practices and ways of working

### Typical Communication
- Change impact assessments and communication plans
- Stakeholder engagement and training materials
- Progress monitoring and adoption metrics
- Executive updates on change readiness

### Interactions
- **With Project Manager:** Aligns change activities with project timeline and milestones
- **With Sponsor/Leadership:** Receives strategic direction and organizational context
- **With Business Analyst:** Understands business process changes and impacts
- **With Developers:** Communicates technical changes and system transitions
- **With Quality Assurance Lead:** Coordinates testing and validation activities
- **With Communications Lead:** Crafts and distributes change messaging

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Leads define quality standards, testing strategies, and processes to ensure deliverables meet requirements and quality expectations. They work throughout the project lifecycle to prevent defects and validate solutions.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality standards and acceptance criteria interpretation
- Develop and maintain test strategies and test plans
- Oversee test execution and defect management
- Identify and communicate quality risks
- Conduct quality reviews and audits
- Establish metrics for quality and test coverage

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality solutions that meet business and technical requirements
- Prevent defects through effective testing and early quality assurance activities
- Maintain customer satisfaction and trust
- Reduce post-release issues and rework

### Typical Communication
- Test plans and test case documentation
- Quality metrics and defect reports
- Risk assessments and quality reviews
- Status updates on testing progress and quality concerns

### Interactions
- **With Developers:** Collaborates on test strategies and participates in code reviews
- **With Project Manager:** Incorporates testing schedules into project timeline
- **With Business Analyst:** Validates and refines acceptance criteria
- **With Solution Architect:** Ensures architectural decisions support testability
- **With Release Manager:** Coordinates testing activities for releases
- **With Agile Coach/Scrum Master:** Participates in ceremonies and process improvement

---

## Agile Coach / Scrum Master

### Role Summary
Agile Coaches and Scrum Masters facilitate agile team processes, remove impediments, and support continuous improvement. They enable teams to work effectively and deliver value incrementally through agile practices.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate agile ceremonies (standups, planning, reviews, retrospectives)
- Remove impediments and blockers that hinder team progress
- Coach teams on agile principles and practices
- Maintain sprint planning and tracking tools
- Identify and drive process improvements
- Support organizational agile transformation initiatives

### Goals
- Enable teams to work efficiently and deliver value incrementally
- Foster team collaboration and psychological safety
- Continuously improve processes and practices
- Support organizational adoption of agile methodologies

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and tracking dashboards
- Retrospective notes and action items
- Team velocity and progress metrics
- Coaching and mentoring interactions

### Interactions
- **With Developers:** Facilitates ceremonies, removes blockers, coaches on practices
- **With Project Manager:** Collaborates on planning and progress tracking
- **With Product Manager:** Ensures prioritized backlog is clear and ready
- **With All Team Members:** Supports agile process adherence and continuous improvement
